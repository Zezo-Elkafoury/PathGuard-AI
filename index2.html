Safety Notice & Scope

PathGuard AI is a research prototype created for science-fair / ISEF-style experimentation.
  • Not a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, or professional mobility aid of any kind.
  • Not a medical device and not certified for navigation or safety-critical use.
  • Object detection is probabilistic: it can miss objects, misclassify them, or misjudge position.
  • Test only in safe, controlled environments — flat, well-lit, obstacle-free rooms with a supervisor present.
  • Audio warnings describe what is visible; they deliberately give no navigation instructions.
  • All processing happens in your browser. No video, audio, or experiment data is uploaded anywhere.
